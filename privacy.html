Privacy Policy

Flip Market for iPhone and iPad. Effective October 14, 2026.

Flip Market is a single-player game made by Christian Kafer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: the game does not collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Flip Market has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information, and the game does not send information about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

Your saved games

Your careers, settings, and progress are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup) may include them, under Apple's terms, not ours.

Internet use

Flip Market works fully offline. The game does not connect to the internet while you play, and it sends nothing about you anywhere.

Purchases

You buy Flip Market through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and any personal information that involves, under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details. The game has no in-app purchases. Money inside the game is pretend money that can't be bought with or turned into real money.

Children

Flip Market does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the game ever starts collecting any data, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
