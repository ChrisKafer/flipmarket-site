Common questions

How do I play with touch?

Tap an icon to open it. Long-press anything for its menu or a hint about what it does. Swipe to scroll lists. Double-tap a window's title bar to make it full screen.

The game only shows a "turn your phone sideways" message or won't rotate.

Flip Market plays in landscape. Turn your device sideways, and check that Rotation Lock is off in Control Center.

Where are my saves? Can I move them to another device?

Saves are kept on your device inside the app. They aren't synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes them. Your device's backup may include them.

There's no sound.

Check the volume and the Silent Mode switch, then the Music and Notifications sliders in the game's Settings.

Can I add my own music, radio stations, or pictures?

Not on iPhone and iPad. Those are PC-only features. The game's own soundtrack plays on every device, and the game works fully offline.

Where's the Neon Chips casino?

The casino is only in the PC version. Flip Market on iPhone and iPad has no gambling of any kind.

I'd like a refund.

Refunds go through Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
